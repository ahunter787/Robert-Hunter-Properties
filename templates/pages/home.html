Phase 0 · Foundation

The RHP portal is being built.

Robert Hunter Properties is moving its tenant and property management onto a self-hosted portal: leases and documents, rent charges and balances, maintenance requests, and announcements — for tenants and for the RHP team.

This deployment currently contains the foundation only: configuration, database, container topology, health checks, and the design system. Tenant sign-in, leases, and rent balances arrive in the phases listed in docs/roadmap.md.

Staff sign-in Service status

What exists today

  • Django + PostgreSQL with split development/production settings
  • Docker Compose topology for a local VM and a cloud VPS
  • Health endpoint, structured logging, tests, and linting
  • RHP design system and responsive base layout

Coming next

  • Phase 2 — properties, units, and tenants
  • Phase 3 — leases
